RE
Researcher / Analyst
External academia, journalism, consultancy
Researcher
Needs data, reports, and methodology documents to cite or validate. No registration, no friction, fast access.
  • Download sector studies & outlook reports
  • Access rating criteria PDFs publicly
  • Find historical press releases by date/sector
  • Understand ACER's methodology vs peers
  • Check SEBI compliance disclosures
IV
Investor / Lender
Existing user MFs, banks, insurance, FIIs
Investor
Tracks ratings on instruments they hold. Needs latest surveillance alerts, upgrades, downgrades. High trust in data accuracy.
  • Find latest rating on a specific entity
  • Check if a rating has been downgraded/on watch
  • Read the full rationale PDF of a rating
  • Track rating actions across their portfolio
  • Subscribe to alerts for specific issuers
NI
New Issuer / Entity
Prospective customer CFO, treasury, corp dev
New customer
Evaluating ACER vs CRISIL/ICRA. Needs to understand cost, process, timeline, and credibility before committing.
  • Understand the rating process step by step
  • See ACER's regulatory standing (SEBI/RBI)
  • Know what sectors & instruments ACER rates
  • Initiate a rating request / get a quote
  • Talk to a relationship manager
RC
Regulator / Compliance Officer
SEBI, RBI, company compliance teams
Regulator
Checks if ACER is filing correctly, maintaining disclosures, and following code of conduct. Needs instant access to mandatory docs.
  • Access SEBI registration certificate
  • Download annual compliance certificate
  • Find conflict of interest policy
  • Locate grievance redressal mechanism
  • Check regulatory filings archive
Designer note persona-led design decisions
  • Researcher and Investor both arrive via Google search for "ACER Ratings [entity name]" or "ACER credit outlook". Landing pages for press releases and research must be SEO-optimised, with immediate PDF access no login wall.
  • New issuer arrives via referral or LinkedIn. The hero section and "Get Rated" CTA are built for them. The rating process page must have a clear, numbered flow + a contact/request form at the end.
  • Regulator arrives directly to the Disclosures section. This section must be accessible within 1 click from the homepage no deep nesting.
  • Newsletter and press release subscription serves primarily the Investor and Researcher personas both want passive updates without checking the site daily.
  • The brand disambiguation problem ("ACER = laptops?") is felt most acutely by the New Issuer hero copy, metadata, and the page title tag must lead with "credit rating agency".

Arrival
How they find ACER		 Google search "ACER Ratings NBFC sector study" or "credit outlook India CRA" Google search "[Entity] ACER rating downgrade" or direct link from a fund system Referral from bank / CA / CFO peer. Sometimes searches "SEBI credit rating agency India SME" Direct type-in or from SEBI's list of registered CRAs
Landing
First page they see		 Research library page or a specific report/press release A specific press release or the Rating Actions table Homepage hero sees "Get Rated" CTA Disclosures section or homepage
Explore
What they look at		 Research → Sector Studies → Economy Outlook → Criteria PDFs Rating Actions table → Entity search → Press release PDF → Rating scale reference About → Who we are → Ratings → Rating Process → Why ACER vs peers Disclosures → SEBI certificate → Code of conduct → Grievance → Annual report
Action
What they do		 Downloads PDF report. May subscribe to newsletter. May cite ACER in their publication. Downloads rating rationale. Sets up alert for entity. Verifies watch/downgrade. Fills the "Get Rated" form or calls the RM. Shares internally for a decision. Downloads compliance certificate. Screenshots grievance mechanism details.
Trust signal needed
What makes them stay		 Public access to criteria docs. Clear methodology. Recent publications (not stale). Up-to-date rating actions. Full rationale PDF available. SEBI/RBI badge visible. Process clarity. Board credibility. Regulatory standing. Client case (if any). Clear fees/timeline. Compliance filings are current. Grievance contact is real and reachable.
Failure mode
Where they drop off		 Reports are old or gated. Too many clicks to find a PDF. No entity search. Rating actions are stale. Rationale PDF behind a form. Process page is vague. No contact point obvious. Can't tell if ACER rates their sector. SEBI certificate is missing or link is broken. Grievance page has no actual contact.
Key design implications from journey map
  • Global search bar is not optional Investor and Researcher both search for entity names. Must be in the sticky top nav.
  • Every press release and research PDF must be directly accessible no registration wall for core regulatory content.
  • The "Get Rated" CTA must appear in hero, sticky nav, and at the bottom of the Ratings section. Not once.
  • Disclosures must be reachable in 1 click from the nav it cannot be buried in a footer link only.
  • Newsletter signup serves both Researcher and Investor passively place it in Research section + footer, not just a popup.
  • Mobile experience must not break the press release / PDF flow Investor often checks rating status on mobile.

Mobile notes
Mobile (375px)
Board card grid collapses to 1-column. Timeline switches to vertical scroll. Backer logos wrap centred. Hero H1 max 2 lines at 20px.
Designer checklist
  • Hero H1 leads with "credit rating agency" not just "ACER Ratings"
  • Brand disambiguation ("not the laptop brand") is visually prominent
  • All board member names are linked to their full bios on the About page
  • SEBI registration number visible near / below the board section
  • Board card grid tested at 375px viewport without overflow
  • Backer logos have text fallbacks for when images fail to load
  • Timeline is interactive (hover states) on desktop, scrollable on mobile
  • Careers subsection links to a dedicated /careers page (not a mailto)
  • All images have alt text with person's name and role
  • Page meta description includes "SEBI-registered credit rating agency India"
📌 Board section is a SEBI requirement names, qualifications, and committee roles must be publicly available. "Backed by" uses logos for recognition; keep names factual and accurate.

Interaction states
Doc cardDefaultShows doc title, last updated date, PDF icon. Hover: org border, download cursor.
Status badgeActiveGreen "Active" with validity date. Turns amber when ≤60 days to renewal. Turns red when expired.
Grievance formDefaultComplaint type, description, contact. Submit → acknowledgement with reference number and 7-day SLA.
Copy guidelines
Section intro copy
Every document SEBI requires us to publish available here, always current. Our commitment to transparency is not just regulatory: it is foundational.
Tone: confident, compliance-forward. Avoid dry legalese. Make it human but authoritative.
Mobile notes
Mobile (375px)
Document cards stack to 1-column. Grievance form is full-width. Filings archive table scrolls horizontally. SEBI SCORES and SMART ODR are large tap targets (min 44px height).
Designer checklist
  • SEBI registration number displayed prominently on Disclosures page and in footer of every page
  • SEBI registration certificate PDF link is functional and opens directly
  • RBI recognition letter PDF link is functional
  • Registration status badge shows "Active" with validity date clear visual state for when it expires
  • Grievance form functional submissions reach Compliance Officer
  • SEBI SCORES link opens https://scores.sebi.gov.in in new tab
  • SMART ODR link opens https://smartodr.in in new tab
  • Compliance Officer's name, email, and phone number are real and verified
  • Code of conduct document shows "last updated" date
  • Annual filings archive includes current FY compliance certificate and default study
  • Conflict of interest policy includes shareholding pattern of ACER Ratings Pvt. Ltd.
  • All disclosure links checked quarterly set calendar reminder for Q1 of each FY
📌 Disclosures is your most legally critical section. Every item is a SEBI regulatory requirement. Broken links or outdated documents = compliance risk. Review quarterly minimum.
