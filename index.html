Researcher / Analyst

Researcher
Needs data, reports, and methodology documents to cite or validate. Doesn't want to register or pay. Fast, no-friction access.
  • Download sector studies & outlook reports
  • Access rating criteria PDFs publicly
  • Find historical press releases by date/sector
  • Understand ACER's methodology vs peers
  • Check SEBI compliance disclosures
IV
Investor / Lender
Existing user — MFs, banks, insurance, FIIs
Investor
Tracks ratings on instruments they hold. Needs latest surveillance alerts, upgrades, downgrades. High trust in data accuracy.
  • Find latest rating on a specific entity
  • Check if a rating has been downgraded/on watch
  • Read the full rationale PDF of a rating
  • Track rating actions across their portfolio
  • Subscribe to alerts for specific issuers
NI
New Issuer / Entity
Prospective customer — CFO, treasury, corp dev
New customer
Evaluating ACER vs CRISIL/ICRA. Needs to understand cost, process, timeline, and credibility before committing. Wants to speak to someone.
  • Understand the rating process step by step
  • See ACER's regulatory standing (SEBI/RBI)
  • Know what sectors & instruments ACER rates
  • Initiate a rating request / get a quote
  • Talk to a relationship manager

Regulator / Compliance Officer
SEBI, RBI, company compliance teams
Regulator
Checks if ACER is filing correctly, maintaining disclosures, and following code of conduct. Needs instant access to mandatory docs.
  • Access SEBI registration certificate
  • Download annual compliance certificate
  • Find conflict of interest policy
  • Locate grievance redressal mechanism
  • Check regulatory filings archive
Designer note — persona-led design decisions
  • Researcher and Investor both arrive via Google — they search "ACER Ratings [entity name]" or "ACER credit outlook". Landing pages for press releases and research must be SEO-optimised, with immediate PDF access — no login wall.
  • New issuer arrives via referral or LinkedIn. The hero section and "Get Rated" CTA are built for them. The rating process page must have a clear, numbered flow + a contact/request form at the end.
  • Regulator arrives directly to the Disclosures section. This section must be accessible within 1 click from the homepage — no deep nesting.
  • Newsletter and press release subscription serves primarily the Investor and Researcher personas — both want passive updates without checking the site daily.
  • The brand disambiguation problem ("ACER = laptops?") is felt most acutely by the New Issuer — hero copy, metadata, and the page title tag must lead with "credit rating agency".

Explore
What they look at		 Research → Sector Studies → Economy Outlook → Criteria PDFs Rating Actions table → Entity search → Press release PDF → Rating scale reference About → Who we are → Ratings section → Rating Process → Why ACER vs peers Disclosures → SEBI certificate → Code of conduct → Grievance → Annual report
Action
What they do		 Downloads PDF report. May subscribe to newsletter. May cite ACER in their publication. Downloads rating rationale. Sets up alert/subscription for entity. Verifies watch/downgrade. Fills the "Get Rated" form or calls the RM. Shares internally for a decision. Downloads compliance certificate. Screenshots grievance mechanism details.
Trust signal needed
What makes them stay		 Public access to criteria docs. Clear methodology. Recent publications (not stale). Up-to-date rating actions. Full rationale PDF available. SEBI/RBI badge visible. Process clarity. Board credibility. Regulatory standing. Client case (if any). Clear fees/timeline. Compliance filings are current. Grievance contact is real and reachable. Code of conduct is specific.
Failure mode
Where they drop off		 Reports are old or gated. Too many clicks to find a PDF. No entity search. Rating actions are stale. Rationale PDF behind a form. Process page is vague. No contact point is obvious. Can't tell if ACER rates their sector. SEBI certificate is missing or link is broken. Grievance page has no actual contact.
Key design implications from journey map
  • Global search bar is not optional — Investor and Researcher both search for entity names. Must be in the sticky top nav.
  • Every press release and research PDF must be directly accessible — no registration wall for core regulatory content.
  • The "Get Rated" CTA must appear in hero, sticky nav, and at the bottom of the Ratings section. Not once.
  • Disclosures must be reachable in 1 click from the nav — it cannot be buried in a footer link only.
  • Newsletter signup serves both Researcher and Investor passively — place it in Research section + footer, not just a popup.
  • Mobile experience must not break the press release / PDF flow — Investor often checks rating status on mobile.

Copy guidelines — About
Hero H1 — recommended frame
"We are ACER Ratings — India's independent credit rating agency."
Lead with "credit rating agency" for disambiguation. "ACER" alone = laptops to many users.
Hero sub-copy — recommended frame
"SEBI-registered. RBI-recognised. Delivering rigorous, conflict-free credit opinions that India's debt markets can depend on."
Two sentences max. Include both SEBI and RBI credentials. Avoid superlatives ("best", "leading").
Mission card
"To enable informed capital allocation by delivering independent, transparent credit opinions — for every corner of India's debt market."
One sentence. Action-oriented. No jargon beyond what a CFO would use.
Careers CTA
"Build India's credit analysis infrastructure — join the team →"
Aspirational framing, not just "apply now". Target: junior analysts and fresh graduates.

Designer checklist — About section

Rating scale component
AAA → D visual bar
  • Horizontal gradient bar: green (AAA) → yellow (BBB) → red (D)
  • Investment grade / speculative grade split: clear visual divider at BB+
  • Each grade is a tooltip target — hover shows: definition + example instrument type
  • Modifier (+/−) shown as smaller text below the main grade label
  • Downloadable PDF button at end: "Download rating scale PDF"
  • SEBI mandate: scale definition must match SEBI CRA regulations exactly
7-step process flow
Rating process timeline
  • Horizontal on desktop, vertical on mobile
  • Steps: 1.Mandate 2.Data 3.Site visit 4.Analysis 5.Committee 6.Decision 7.Surveillance
  • Each step: numbered circle (orange), title, 1-line description
  • Connecting line between steps (dashed or solid)
  • Step 5 (Committee) should note: "Independent — issuer has no influence"
  • Timeline commitment: "First rating in 30 working days" visible prominently
Rating actions table
Filterable live data table
  • Columns: Date | Entity | Instrument | Previous | Current | Action | PDF
  • Action badge colours: Upgrade=green, Downgrade=red, Watch=amber, New=blue, Reaffirmed=grey
  • Filter row above table: Action type dropdown + Sector dropdown + Date range picker
  • Search bar: entity name search (client-side filter or API)
  • PDF icon in last column — direct download, no login
  • Pagination: 20 rows/page. "Load more" or numbered pagination
  • Empty state: "No results for [filter]. Try adjusting the filters."
Get rated form
Lead capture + RM contact
  • Fields: Company name, Instrument type (dropdown), Facility size (₹ Cr range), Sector (dropdown), Name, Email, Phone
  • Max 7 fields — every extra field reduces submission rate
  • Below form: RM name, photo, phone, email — for users who prefer direct contact
  • Success state: confirmation message + "expect a call within 1 business day"
  • SEBI compliance note below form: "ACER Ratings is a SEBI-registered CRA (Reg. No. XXXXX)"
  • Form placement: end of Ratings section + standalone /get-rated page

"1. Mandate letter → 2. Data submission → 3. Management meeting → 4. Analyst assessment → 5. Rating committee → 6. Rating disclosure → 7. Annual surveillance"

Vary the copy per placement. Never repeat the exact same CTA string twice on one page.
Mobile notes — Ratings
